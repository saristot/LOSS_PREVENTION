## Method
The DONE items in `roadmap.txt` are titles without acceptance criteria. For each item:
- **Implemented**: code evidence of what exists.
- **R – missing vs. requirement**: the feature declared in the title does not work, or works only partially.
- **Q – quality assessment**: robustness, security, architecture or completeness beyond the title. This is the auditor's assessment, **not requested by the roadmap**. Several of these topics are explicitly deferred by the roadmap itself: "Security Hardening", "API - Permissions - Review", "Refactor", "Unit Tests", "Login - Forgot Password, Will required an email service", "Extend Functionality for Alerts and Notifications", "Dashboard - Lock down to region/store/district", "KNN, ensure functionality is working as expected".
- **Result**: based **only on column R**. ✅ requirement met · 🟡 requirement partially met · ❌ requirement not implemented.
